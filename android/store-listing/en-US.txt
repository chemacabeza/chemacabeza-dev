Full description
Explore the articles and projects of José María Cabeza Rodríguez, an engineering manager and software engineer.

Read about software engineering and system design, browse selected projects, and learn about his professional background and interests. Find links to his open-source work, newsletter, and contact options.

The app opens the current chemacabeza.dev website in an Android app window. An internet connection is required to load the website. Some links open external services, including meeting booking and a separate digital-model marketplace. Certain services and products available through those links require payment.
